Privacy Policy

Genex Intl Pvt. Ltd. respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you browse our store or place an order, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We only collect what's needed to run the store and fulfil your orders:

  • Account details — your name, email, phone number, and password.
  • Delivery details — shipping address, billing address, and order notes.
  • Order history — products purchased, prices paid, payment status.
  • Browsing activity — pages visited, items viewed, basic device info (used to improve the site and detect fraud).

2. How we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders for refrigerators, washing machines, and other appliances.
  • To send order confirmations, delivery updates, and warranty reminders.
  • To respond to your enquiries and after-sales service requests.
  • To improve our website, product range, and customer support.
  • To comply with Nepali tax, accounting, and consumer-protection laws.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with:

  • Delivery partners — name, address, and phone number, so they can deliver your appliance.
  • Payment processors (eSewa, Khalti, FonePay, banks) — so they can authorise your payment.
  • Service technicians — when a warranty or repair visit is requested.
  • Government authorities — only where required by Nepali law.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your cart, and understand which pages and products are popular. You can clear cookies in your browser settings — note that doing so may log you out and empty your cart.

5. Your rights

  • You can view and update your account details on your profile page.
  • You can request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to delete your account, subject to records we must keep for tax / warranty purposes.
Heads-up on warranty records

If you've purchased an appliance from us, we keep your order record for the duration of the manufacturer's warranty even after account closure, so we can honour service claims.

6. Security

We store passwords hashed (never in plain text), use HTTPS across the entire site, and limit internal access to your data on a need-to-know basis. No system is perfectly secure — use a strong, unique password for your account.

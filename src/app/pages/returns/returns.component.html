Returns & Refunds

We want you to be confident about every appliance you buy from us. If something's not right, here's exactly what you can do — and what we'll do.

1. Damaged or wrong item on arrival

Inspect your refrigerator or washing machine as soon as the courier hands it over. If you spot transit damage or the wrong model has been delivered, contact us within 24 hours of delivery. We'll arrange a free pick-up and either send a replacement or issue a full refund.

What you'll need to send

Photos of the appliance and the packaging, your order number, and a brief description of the issue. WhatsApp / email both work.

2. Manufacturing defect within 7 days

If the appliance powers on but isn't working as it should, you have 7 days from delivery to report the defect. We'll send a technician to confirm the fault. Once confirmed, you can choose:

  • A free repair, or
  • A replacement of the same model, or
  • A full refund if neither of the above is feasible.

3. Change-of-mind returns

Large appliances are not eligible for change-of-mind returns once they have been used. If the appliance is still sealed in its original packaging, you may return it within 3 days of delivery — a 10% restocking fee plus return shipping applies.

4. After the first week — warranty service

From day 8 onwards, faults are handled under the manufacturer's warranty. The warranty duration and coverage are printed on your invoice and warranty card. Reach out to us and we'll schedule the service visit — you don't have to chase the manufacturer yourself.

5. What's not eligible for return

  • Appliances damaged by voltage surges, water ingress, or physical impact after delivery.
  • Units that have been opened, modified, or serviced by an unauthorised technician.
  • Accessories and consumables (filters, hoses, detergent trays) once opened.
  • Products bought on clearance / final-sale (clearly marked at the time of purchase).

6. Refund timeline

  • Original payment method (eSewa / Khalti / card / FonePay): 5–7 business days after the item is received back and inspected.
  • Cash on delivery: bank transfer to your account, 7–10 business days.

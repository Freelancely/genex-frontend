Shipping Policy

Refrigerators and washing machines are heavy, fragile, and worth protecting in transit. Here's how we get your appliance from our warehouse to your door — anywhere in Nepal.

Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

1. Delivery zones

  • Kathmandu Valley (Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Bhaktapur) — free delivery on all appliances above NPR 25,000.
  • Major cities outside the valley (Pokhara, Chitwan, Biratnagar, Butwal, Nepalgunj, Birgunj, Dharan, Hetauda, Itahari, Janakpur) — delivery charge calculated at checkout based on appliance size.
  • Other districts — delivery available via partnered logistics. We'll confirm cost and time once you place the order.

2. Delivery time

  • Kathmandu Valley: 1–2 business days
  • Major cities: 3–5 business days
  • Other districts: 5–10 business days

Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed the next working day.

3. Delivery charges

Delivery cost depends on the appliance size, weight, and your district. The exact amount is shown at checkout before payment — no surprises.

Free delivery promo

All orders above NPR 25,000 inside Kathmandu Valley ship free. The discount is applied automatically at checkout.

4. What we deliver

Our courier brings your appliance to your doorstep. They will:

  • Carry the product to your ground-floor entrance.
  • Unbox it with you and confirm there's no physical damage.
  • Hand you the original invoice and warranty card.

Upper-floor delivery, installation, and old-unit pick-up are not included by default. If you need any of these, please mention it in your order notes and we'll quote the extra charge.

5. Tracking your order

You can see the status of every order (placed, packed, dispatched, delivered) on your order history page. We also send SMS updates to the phone number on the order.

6. Receiving the appliance

  • Please be available at the delivery address during business hours on the scheduled day.
  • Inspect the unit before signing the delivery acknowledgement — any transit damage must be reported then and there.
  • If you're not home and the courier can't reach you after two attempts, the order returns to our warehouse. Re-delivery charges may apply.

7. Damaged or missing items

If your appliance arrives damaged or with missing parts, contact us within 24 hours of delivery with photos. We will arrange a replacement or full refund — see the Returns Policy for details.
